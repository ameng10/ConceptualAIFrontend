Case studies

Five applications, each generated from one prompt. For every one of them the full prompt is published, the app is live and usable, and both repositories are public. They are listed here so you can check the claim rather than take it.

All twelve repositories have exactly one commit each — "Initial export from ConceptualAI". None has been touched since the day it was generated. That is the cheapest claim in the world to disprove and the reason it is worth making: open any of them and look at the history.

  • Deskmind — Also our real support desk
    An AI support inbox: automatic triage, draft replies grounded in your own help docs, and real two-way email — on a backend you own.
  • Atelier
    A visual booking marketplace: photo listings, schedules that can’t double-book, and a natural-language booking assistant.
  • Commons — v1 ⇄ v2 — one iteration apart
    A paid community with Stripe supporter subscriptions — then one iteration adds AI moderation without touching the billing.
  • Stride
    An AI training coach that shows its sources: web-research-grounded plans that adapt to your daily feedback.
  • Shopfront
    A real store: cart checkout with Stripe, inventory that can’t double-decrement, and confirmation emails sent exactly once.

What these are meant to show

Not that the apps are beautiful — that they are real. Each has a backend doing actual work: authentication, a database, payments or email or AI where the prompt asked for it. That is the part an interface-first generator does not give you, and the part a development shop would charge you for.

If you are weighing options, the comparison of app builders, dev shops, and us is more useful than any single case study, and it is honest about when we are the wrong choice.
